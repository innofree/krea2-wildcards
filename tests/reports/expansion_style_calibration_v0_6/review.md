# Style prompt calibration v0.6

Status: failed prompt calibration; excluded from lifecycle scoring.

Five deliberately separated five-axis candidates were run at seeds 1001, 2002, and 3003. All 15
runs produced valid 1024 × 1024 images with one adult subject, complete framing, stable plain
clothing, coherent anatomy, and no visible text, logo, or watermark. Queue ownership and run-record
checks passed.

The selected shape, color, surface, atmosphere, and edge combinations did not create sufficiently
distinct images. Most samples converged on a neutral, nearly monochrome photorealistic studio result;
even warm-cool, jewel/duotone, ceremonial/nocturnal, fibrous, and edge-specific instructions were
weak or absent. These runs prove scene-control stability but fail the style-effectiveness purpose.
They must not be merged into an official scorecard or used for promotion.

The next revision replaces abstract treatment language with direct, observable frame-level
instructions, removes the benchmark's neutral-lighting conflict, avoids duplicated quality suffixes,
and requires the selected style to control palette, tonal divisions, surface, atmosphere, and edges.
