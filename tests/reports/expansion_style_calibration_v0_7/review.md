# Observable style calibration v0.7

Status: calibration passed; official screening may proceed.

Five separated five-axis candidates were run at seeds 1001, 2002, and 3003. All 15 runs produced
valid 1024 × 1024 images with one adult subject, complete framing, fixed wardrobe shape and pose,
coherent anatomy, and no visible text, logo, or watermark. The remote queue was empty afterward.

The revised prompts produced stable, observable differences:

- faceted/warm-cool/chalk/ceremonial/crisp: crossing amber and blue beams with angular divisions;
- slender/duotone/satin/nocturnal/lost-found: narrow low-key illumination and dark merging edges;
- minimal/mineral/weathered/contemplative/soft: quiet muted fields with restrained texture;
- ornamental/mineral/fibrous/contemplative/etched: repeated decorative framing and print texture;
- rounded/warm-cool/fibrous/nocturnal/soft: a large circular light field and warm-cool separation.

The muted minimal candidate is deliberately subtler than the others, while the ornamental frame can
reduce the subject's relative size; neither caused a critical scene-control failure. This calibration
validates the prompt design and does not itself score, approve, or promote any catalog item.
